## Your files are your responsibility

MTP Commander changes files only when you ask it to. Some of those changes cannot be undone.

- **Deleting** removes files and folders from your phone, USB devices and USB drives permanently. Only items deleted in Google Drive go to Drive's trash.
- **Replacing** an item that already exists at the destination deletes the existing one, after you confirm it.
- **Copying to a device** such as a watch or bike computer writes to that device's storage. Files in the wrong place or format can confuse the device's own software.
- **Interrupted transfers**, for example when a cable is pulled or a device disconnects, can leave incomplete files behind.

Keep backups of files that matter to you before copying, replacing or deleting them, especially on devices you cannot easily restore.
