MTP Commander is a two-pane file manager for moving files between your phone and the devices you plug into it.

Connect a Garmin watch, another MTP device or a USB drive over USB-C or OTG, and copy files in either direction: maps, courses, workouts, music or FIT activity files. You can also copy files to and from your own Google Drive.

TWO PANES, ANY SOURCE
Open phone storage, a USB device, a USB drive or Google Drive in each pane, and copy from one to the other. Garmin watches show up by their model name.

COPY LISTS, NOT ACCIDENTS
Tap files to add them to a copy list or a trash list, and see the total size before you start. Nothing is copied or deleted until you press the button. If a file already exists at the destination, the app asks before overwriting it.

FIND FILES FAST
Search every subfolder by name or pattern, such as *.fit or course?.gpx, and add the results straight to your list. Sort any folder by name, size or date.

BUILT FOR RELIABLE TRANSFERS
- Progress shown for every copy
- Drag and drop between panes
- Opens the device as soon as you grant USB access
- Closes a pane cleanly when a drive is unplugged
- Night mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no ads, no analytics. Google Drive access is limited to files MTP Commander created itself, so the app never sees the rest of your Drive.

Requires a phone with USB host (OTG) support.
